How Larder & Ledger helps

One app for your kitchen and your shopping

Spend less

Before you shop, it prices your list at the supermarkets you use, with your own loyalty prices, and shows what sticking with one is costing you. You decide where to buy.

Waste less

It knows what’s in your fridge, freezer and cupboard, and nudges you to use things before they go off. Less food in the bin, and less carbon behind it.

Eat better

New recipes built from what you already have, to break the five-dinner rut, with nutrition tracked from what you buy and cook. No food diary needed.
